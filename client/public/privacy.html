Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

⚠️ Draft — pending legal review. This document is provided in good faith and will be finalised with qualified legal counsel. It is not yet a final legal agreement.

1. Who we are

VidyaSetu ("we", "us") provides software that academies use to manage students, attendance, fees, lessons and parent communication. This policy explains how we handle personal data. For any privacy question or request, contact us at support@veena.app.

2. Our role

Each academy that uses VidyaSetu decides what data to collect about its students and families and why — the academy is the data fiduciary (controller). VidyaSetu processes that data only to provide the service, on the academy's instructions — we act as a data processor on the academy's behalf.

3. What we collect

Depending on how an academy uses VidyaSetu, the platform may store:

  • Student details — name, date of birth, photo (optional), and academic records
  • Parent/guardian contact — name, phone number, email address
  • Attendance, fees, payments and related notes
  • Lessons, assignments, quiz results and certificates
  • Account details for people who sign in (email, encrypted password, role)
  • Basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service

4. Why we process it

Only to provide and support the service the academy has asked for — recording attendance, calculating fees, sending reminders and updates, delivering lessons, and keeping accounts secure. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Children's data

Students are often minors. Their data is processed on behalf of the academy, which is responsible for obtaining verifiable parental or guardian consent before adding a child's details. We do not knowingly use children's data for tracking or targeted advertising. Parents may ask their academy, or contact us, to review or remove a child's information.

6. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers ("sub-processors") purely to operate the service:

  • Supabase — database and file storage
  • Netlify — application hosting
  • Resend — sending account and notification emails
  • Web-push services — delivering notifications you opt into

We share data with these providers only as needed to run VidyaSetu. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

7. How long we keep it

We keep data for as long as the academy uses the service. Old, non-essential records (such as expired notifications) are cleaned up automatically. When an academy leaves, or on a valid request, we delete or return the relevant data within a reasonable period.

8. Security

Each academy's data is kept logically separate, access requires authentication, data is encrypted in transit, and backups run daily. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information entrusted to us.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. As most data is controlled by the academy, the quickest route is usually to ask your academy directly; you can also contact us at support@veena.app and we will assist the academy in responding.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The "last updated" date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
